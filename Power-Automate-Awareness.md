# Power Automate Awareness Training Agenda (90 minutes)

**Audience:** Users with no prior Power Automate experience  
**License:** Power Automate for Microsoft 365  
**Format:** Presentation with live demo  
**Duration:** 1.5 Hours  

---

## Agenda Overview

| Segment | Topic | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome & Introduction | 5 min |
| 2 | What is Power Automate? | 10 min |
| 3 | Types of Flows | 15 min |
| 4 | Common Use Cases & Scenarios | 10 min |
| 5 | Demo: Building Your First Flow | 25 min |
| 6 | Getting Started & Best Practices | 10 min |
| 7 | Resources & Next Steps | 10 min |
| 8 | Q&A Session | 5 min |

---

## 1. Welcome & Introduction (5 minutes)
- Introductions and session objectives
- What attendees will learn

## 2. What is Power Automate? (10 minutes)
- Overview of Microsoft Power Platform
- Power Automate definition and purpose
- Key benefits: automation, productivity, and efficiency
- Real-world business impact

## 3. Types of Flows (15 minutes)
- **Cloud flows** (automated, instant, scheduled)
- **Desktop flows** (RPA - Robotic Process Automation)
- **Business process flows**
- When to use each type

## 4. Common Use Cases & Scenarios (10 minutes)
- Approval workflows
- Notification systems
- Data collection and synchronization
- Document management
- Integration examples (Teams, SharePoint, Outlook, etc.)

## 5. Demo: Building Your First Flow (25 minutes)
- Live demonstration of creating a simple automated flow
- Example: "Send email notification when new item added to SharePoint list"
- Walk through the designer interface
- Show connectors and actions
- Test the flow
- Q&A during demo

## 6. Getting Started & Best Practices (10 minutes)
- How to access Power Automate (licensing overview)
- Power Automate portal navigation
- Templates vs. building from scratch
- Best practices for naming and organizing flows

## 7. Resources & Next Steps (10 minutes)
- Microsoft Learn training paths
- Power Automate community
- Templates gallery
- How to request access/licenses in your organization

## 8. Q&A Session (5 minutes)
- Open floor for questions
- Gather feedback

**Note:** This agenda is designed for complete beginners and balances conceptual understanding with hands-on demonstration, ensuring participants leave with both awareness of Power Automate's capabilities and practical knowledge of how it works.
