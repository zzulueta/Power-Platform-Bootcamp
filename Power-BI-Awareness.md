# Power BI Awareness Training Agenda (90 minutes)

**Audience:** Users with no prior Power BI experience  
**License:** Power BI Pro or Microsoft 365  
**Format:** Presentation with live demo  
**Duration:** 1.5 Hours  

---

## Agenda Overview

| Segment | Topic | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome & Introduction | 5 min |
| 2 | What is Power BI? | 10 min |
| 3 | Key Components of Power BI | 15 min |
| 4 | Common Use Cases & Scenarios | 10 min |
| 5 | Demo: Building Your First Report | 25 min |
| 6 | Getting Started & Best Practices | 10 min |
| 7 | Resources & Next Steps | 10 min |
| 8 | Q&A Session | 5 min |

---

## 1. Welcome & Introduction (5 minutes)
- Introductions and session objectives
- What attendees will learn

## 2. What is Power BI? (10 minutes)
- Overview of Microsoft Power Platform
- Power BI definition and purpose
- Key benefits: data-driven decisions, self-service analytics, and collaboration
- Real-world business impact

## 3. Key Components of Power BI (15 minutes)
- **Power BI Desktop** – build and design reports (Windows app)
- **Power BI Service** – publish, share, and collaborate in the browser
- **Power BI Mobile** – view reports on the go
- **Power BI Report Builder** – pixel-perfect paginated reports
- When to use each component

## 4. Common Use Cases & Scenarios (10 minutes)
- Sales and revenue dashboards
- Operational and KPI tracking
- Financial reporting and forecasting
- HR and headcount analytics
- Integration examples (Excel, SharePoint, SQL, Dynamics 365, Teams)

## 5. Demo: Building Your First Report (25 minutes)
- Live demonstration of connecting to a data source (e.g., Excel or SharePoint list)
- Walk through the Power BI Desktop interface
- Drag-and-drop visuals: bar chart, card, slicer
- Publish to the Power BI Service
- Share a report and pin visuals to a dashboard
- Q&A during demo

## 6. Getting Started & Best Practices (10 minutes)
- How to access Power BI (licensing overview: Free, Pro, Premium)
- Power BI Service workspace navigation
- Using templates vs. building from scratch
- Best practices: naming conventions, data refresh scheduling, row-level security basics

## 7. Resources & Next Steps (10 minutes)
- Microsoft Learn training paths
- Power BI community and forums
- Sample datasets and template gallery
- How to request access/licenses in your organization

## 8. Q&A Session (5 minutes)
- Open floor for questions
- Gather feedback
